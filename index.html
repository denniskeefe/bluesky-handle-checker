Bluesky Username Enumerator

Check a list of handles against Bluesky and see which exist. Runs entirely in your browser against the public Bluesky AppView API; there is no backend, and the only requests made are the lookups themselves.

Press Ctrl/Cmd + Enter to run. Bare names get the default suffix appended. Entries containing a dot are treated as full handles (custom domains). Leading @ and bsky.app/profile/ URLs are stripped. Email addresses are reduced to the part before the @; any +tag is dropped, and since bsky.social names cannot contain dots or underscores, both a joined (johndoe) and hyphenated (john-doe) variant are checked.
